Make the shot simpler

Less calculation. More confidence.

Bump & Run Golf turns two easy-to-estimate distances into a clear club recommendation. It is designed for golfers who want to get the ball on the ground quickly and let it roll toward the hole.

Features

A fast tool for a specific shot

01

Quick club selection

Receive a recommended wedge or iron based on the relationship between carry distance and rollout distance.

02

Landing-spot guidance

Use the app to understand where the ball should land before it begins rolling toward the target.

03

Simple visual workflow

A focused interface provides the information you need without statistics, accounts, advertisements, or unnecessary menus.

04

Works offline

No login or internet connection is required. The calculation happens locally on your iPhone.
