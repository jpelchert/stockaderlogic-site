Privacy Policy

Bump & Run Golf is designed to work without collecting personal information.

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Information We Collect

Bump & Run Golf does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

Accounts

The app does not require users to create an account or sign in.

Analytics

The app does not use analytics services or track how users interact with the app.

Advertising

The app does not display advertising and does not use advertising identifiers.

Third Parties

The app does not sell, share, or disclose personal information to third parties.

Internet Connection

The app's core functionality operates locally on the user's device and does not require an internet connection.

Children's Privacy

Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy may be sent to stockaderlogic@gmail.com .
